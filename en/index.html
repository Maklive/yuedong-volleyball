Tournament Info

📋

Registration & Check-in

Check in at the Jingxiu Vocational Technical School registration desk between 09:00 and 17:00 on 31 December for eligibility review, equipment inspection and accommodation sign-in. Team leaders' and coaches' meeting at 16:30, group draw at 17:45.

Fill in the entry form here Tencent Docs form (Chinese) ↗
🏐

Divisions

U12, U15 and U18, each with a boys' and a girls' division — six divisions, eight teams each, 48 teams in total. Round-robin groups, crossover knockout and placement matches.

Division details
📅

Schedule & Results

After the opening ceremony on 1 January, 12 rounds of group matches are played and 72 matches completed that day. Crossover knockout and placement matches follow on 2 January; all six finals start together at 15:00, with the medal ceremony at 16:10.

View Full Schedule
🏨

Accommodation & Transport

Three designated hotels, each paired with a venue: U18 at Taishan New World Hotel (Jingxiu Vocational School), U15 at Fairfield by Marriott Changdachang (Taishan No.1 Middle School), U12 at Wenhua Yashi Hotel (Donghui Primary School). Shuttles run to the match schedule.

On-site medical support, sports-injury prevention guidance and safeguarding rules for underage players — looking after every young athlete.

Closing Night · 2 Jan, 16:10

Medal Ceremony · The Moment of Glory

At 15:00 on 2 January all six finals start at once across six courts; the U18 boys' and girls' finals are played on Courts 1 and 2 at Jingxiu Vocational School and streamed live. At 16:10 everyone moves to Court 1 as the final standings are announced and medals and trophies awarded — closing out the tournament.

Three Phases

Three Phases

PHASE 01

Main Tournament

31 Dec – 2 Jan
📍 Taishan
  • 31 Dec: Check-in and eligibility review (09:00–17:00), team leaders' and coaches' meeting at 16:30, group draw at 17:45
  • 1 Jan: Opening ceremony at 07:20 (Court 1, Jingxiu Vocational School), play starts at 08:00, 12 rounds of group matches until 20:00
  • 2 Jan: Crossover knockout and placement matches from 08:00, six finals at 15:00, medal ceremony at 16:10
PHASE 02

Training Camp

3 – 8 Jan
📍 Taishan
  • Mornings: focused training on technique and tactics
  • Afternoons: friendlies or joint practice with local Taishan school teams
  • One day of sightseeing in Taishan, the hometown of volleyball
PHASE 03

International Friendlies

9 – 10 Jan
📍 Macao, China
  • Hosting the Australian team in Macao, China
  • A two-day international friendly series
  • The closing event, followed by a cultural exchange
